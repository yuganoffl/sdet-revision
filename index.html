Nine lessons, from the first driver call to the rapid-fire answers.

Lesson 1Your first Selenium testOpen a browser. Find one thing on the page. Do something to it. Check the result. Close the browser. Every Selenium test you ever write is that loop, and by the end of this page you will have run it four times.Lesson 2WaitsThe page is not ready and your test does not know it. This is the single biggest source of flaky UI tests, and "explain implicit versus explicit versus fluent" is close to guaranteed in an interview. Every number on this page was measured on your machine, on Selenium 4.21.0.Lesson 3OOP in your frameworkInterviewers ask "explain the four pillars" and get a textbook definition back from everybody. The answer that stands out names the pillar, then points at a line of framework code. This lesson gives you both halves for each one.Lesson 4Explaining your frameworkTwo questions, one structure. "Tell me about your framework" and "how would you build one from scratch" are the same six layers, told forwards or backwards. Learn the layers and you can answer either, plus every follow-up they hang off them.Lesson 5Exception handlingTwo halves. The Java rules, which they will ask you cold. Then the Selenium catalogue: what each one means, why it happened, and the fix. Every exception below is thrown for real by a test in this repo, and the class hierarchy was read out of the jar rather than remembered.Lesson 6LocatorsEight ways to find an element. You will use two of them. This lesson covers all eight quickly, then spends its time on CSS and XPath, which is where the real skill is. Every selector on this page ran green in LocatorsTest.Lesson 7Element typesMost of the page is findElement then click or sendKeys. Five things are not, and those five are what interviewers ask about. This lesson is those five, plus tables and the JavaScript escape hatch. All of it ran green in ElementTypesTest.Lesson 8ScreenshotsShort lesson, and the one where the two courses meet. TestNG lesson 7 told you onTestFailure is where the screenshot goes. This is the other half: what actually takes it, and why the listener cannot reach your driver unless you have built for it. All measured on this machine.Lesson 9Rapid-fire answersThe rapid-fire round. Each one is the short answer, two lines of why, and the code. Read it top to bottom once, then cover the answers and work down the list.
